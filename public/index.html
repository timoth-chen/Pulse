When do my payroll contributions stop?

2026 · one employer · employee CPP/EI outside Quebec · full-year coverage. Enter year-to-date figures from your latest pay stub. Projections assume every future pay has the same pensionable and insurable gross.

CPP and CPP2 have separate limits. They reset each calendar year. Pay dates are estimates; employer rounding and exempt periods can change the last deduction.

CRA CPP / CPP2 / EI limits ↗

How much room is left?

2026 worksheet · use your verified opening room and complete contribution records across every institution. Account balances and investment gains do not tell us your room.

TFSA withdrawals restore room next January, not immediately. FHSA: standard room only, no special re-participation/excess corrections. Qualifying institution-to-institution transfers are not new TFSA contributions.

How this budget works

Set a monthly limit for a category and owner. Actual purchases reduce what is left, including expenses logged before your CSV arrives. Matching those expenses during import avoids counting them twice. Credit-card payments and money transfers do not count as spending again.

Fixed, Flexible and Non-monthly are planning groups. You can override a single month, and optional rollover carries both unused money and overspending forward. Refunds reduce spending. Bills are upcoming commitments; they are not subtracted here until the purchase is recorded. Income here is recorded income, not predicted pay.
